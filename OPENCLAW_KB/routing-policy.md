# Standard Guidance & Routing Policy

## Purpose
Ensure every action, response, and automated workflow routinely refers to (and is traceable through) formal workspace rules and documentation for consistency, auditability, and user transparency.

## Policy
- At every key decision point, agents/subagents must:
  - Check and refer back to rules, policies, and project governance docs in OPENCLAW_KB/.
  - Reference relevant sections of BOOTSTRAP.md, LONG_TERM_MEMORY.md, <PROJECT>.md, or ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.md when issuing responses or taking actions.
  - Cite Knowledgebase docs by their filenames for every policy, naming, security, or operational question to ensure no context drift.
- All agent/subagent operations must treat LONG_TERM_MEMORY.md as the canonical source for persistent memory, agent/project mapping, and rules.
- All responses must be routed through, and checked against, this standard policy and guidance structure.
