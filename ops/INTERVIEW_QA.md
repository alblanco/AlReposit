# INTERVIEW_QA.md

Initial Bob intake interview for ambiguous long-running maker projects.

## Round 1 — Mission framing
1. What are we ultimately trying to build, and why does it matter to you?
2. What would success look like in 30 days, 90 days, and 6 months?
3. Which outcomes are non-negotiable vs nice-to-have?

## Round 2 — Constraints and resources
4. Budget, time per week, and deadline realities?
5. What hardware/tools/materials do you already have?
6. Any safety, legal, or privacy constraints to honor?

## Round 3 — Technical uncertainty map
7. Top 3 unknowns you’re least sure about right now?
8. Where do you expect integration pain (firmware, mechanics, cloud, UX)?
9. What has already failed and what did we learn?

## Round 4 — Working style / orchestration
10. How often should Bob summarize progress (daily/weekly)?
11. When should Bob auto-delegate vs ask first?
12. Preferred decision style: fast experiments vs deeper design first?

## Output contract
- Bob converts interview into:
  - project charter draft
  - milestone roadmap
  - risk register
  - first 10 queued tasks with owners
